# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4 + TypeScript. Already scaffolded via create-next-app; this stack is fixed by the existing codebase.

## Users

Two audiences, one primary for this surface:

- **Primary (conversion target): fashion clients** — brands, casting directors, and stylists who need to cast models for shows, campaigns, and fittings. The landing page's job is to earn their trust and get them to make a casting/client request.
- **Secondary: aspiring and working models** — women (Lei) and men (Lui) seeking representation, who reach the agency through open casting (Casting aperto).

## Product Purpose

LIINE is a model management agency. Its stated reason for existing: *"LIINE nasce per cambiare il modo in cui la moda seleziona i modelli"* — to change the way fashion selects models. Success is a client submitting a casting/client request and, over time, choosing LIINE as their agency of record.

## Positioning

Precision-based selection over generic measurements. LIINE presents talent by real garment fit — treating each casting request like bespoke tailoring rather than matching against predetermined sizes. Models are described as "accuratamente selezionati" (carefully selected). This fit-first mechanism is the differentiator a neighboring roster-first agency could not truthfully copy.

## Product Principles

1. **Fit over roster.** Lead with the precision/bespoke-fit mechanism, not just a wall of faces — it is the reason LIINE exists.
2. **Client-first surface.** The landing page must move a fashion client toward a casting/client request; model-recruitment (open casting) is present but secondary.
3. **Source real content.** Every model, name, contact channel, and claim comes from the live site or the user — never invented.
4. **Quiet authority.** The agency's credibility is carried by restraint and craft (minimal, precise), consistent with the model-agency category and the stated Elite-inspired direction.
